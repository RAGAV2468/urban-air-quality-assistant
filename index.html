About Airwise

Understand the reading. Choose your next step.

Airwise brings city-level air-quality data and clear, cautious guidance into one simple view.

Where the data comes from

Air-quality and geocoding information are provided by Open-Meteo. The dashboard shows the provider’s US AQI scale and pollutant concentrations for the selected coordinates.

Modelled data · not a street sensor

How to read the forecast

The hourly chart is an estimate for the next 24 hours. Local conditions can differ, and forecasts can change as new data arrives.

What the map shows

The interactive street map helps you orient yourself around the selected city. The AQI marker represents the location used for the city-level reading, not a sensor station or pollution boundary.

Health guidance

Suggestions in Airwise are general and educational. They do not diagnose conditions or replace advice from a qualified health professional or local public-health authority.
